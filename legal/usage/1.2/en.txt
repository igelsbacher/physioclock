Use & Privacy

Timer starts in neutral timer mode by default. Optional movement exercises are enabled using a locally stored master switch. Programmes, sets, history, weekly plans and recovery state are stored separately for the two modes. If optional iCloud sync is enabled, the profiles are stored in separate records in the same private CloudKit database. Photo selection is local. The mode switch itself is not synced. Training-data exports and deletions apply to the currently active mode.

Timer is the new display name of the former PhysioClock app. The custom display name is saved locally per device. New wellbeing entries can no longer be recorded. Existing wellbeing data and notes remain in existing data and exports until deleted; they may still synchronise when iCloud sync is enabled. They can be removed using “Delete all training data”.

Timer helps you time your own movement routines. Choose exercises and durations suited to your abilities. The app does not replace medical advice or treatment.

Your training data and optional wellbeing entries are stored locally. If you enable iCloud sync, app data is also stored in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database. Timer uses no advertising or advertising tracking.

This does not enable iCloud sync, Apple Health, notifications or widgets. These features remain optional and are enabled separately.

You accept the linked usage notices and acknowledge the privacy information. These text versions, language, app version and device time are recorded. This does not certify that you read every word.
