Quick start↓
Explore every feature

All features are included for your first 5 started training sessions. Continue afterwards with a one-time Premium unlock in the app. No subscription.

5 free sessions

Start training in 1 minute

  1. Open the app

    Open PhysioClock and go to the training overview. On first launch, choose Simple or Full mode.

  2. Go to training

    Tap “Open training” (Simple mode) or “Continue to training” (Full mode). You can start with the programme shown.

  3. Start the timer

    Tap “Start”. The timer begins with the first exercise and moves on automatically.
