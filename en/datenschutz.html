Privacy

Version 1.2 · 2026-11-19

Timer starts in neutral timer mode by default. Optional movement exercises are enabled using a locally stored master switch. Programmes, sets, history, weekly plans and recovery state are stored separately for the two modes. If optional iCloud sync is enabled, the profiles are stored in separate records in the same private CloudKit database. Photo selection is local. The mode switch itself is not synced. Training-data exports and deletions apply to the currently active mode.

Timer is the new display name of the former PhysioClock app. The custom display name is saved locally per device. New wellbeing entries can no longer be recorded. Existing wellbeing data and notes remain in existing data and exports until deleted; they may still synchronise when iCloud sync is enabled. They can be removed using “Delete all training data”.

At a glance

Your training data remains available locally. iCloud sync is optional. Timer uses no advertising or advertising tracking. Acknowledging the usage notes does not give blanket consent to optional processing.

Data and purposes

The app processes exercises, sets, training duration and history, goals, weekly plans, favourites, settings and reminder times to organise training and display progress. Optional wellbeing entries and notes provide a personal overview. They are not used to produce diagnoses or medical recommendations. No separate Timer account is required.

Free text and wellbeing

Exercise names, set names and notes may contain personal or health-related information. Enter only what you need for your overview and avoid information about other people. Free text is stored like other app data and synchronised when sync is enabled; JSON exports may include it. Entering text does not consent to advertising, research or medical analysis.

Optional iCloud sync

When enabled, exercises, sets, history, wellbeing and notes, goals, weekly plans, favourites, training settings and reminder times are stored in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database and synchronised across your devices. Change timestamps and a generated device identifier resolve conflicts. No separate Timer server stores this data. Turning sync off stops app synchronisation but does not delete existing cloud data. iOS or iCloud device backups are separate and depend on your Apple settings.

Apple Health, notifications and displays

After separate permission, Apple Health is read only to display today’s steps. Timer writes no Health data and does not include the step reading in its history, exports or app iCloud sync. Local notifications require device permission. Optional widgets use training minutes, time goal and update time locally. Live Activities show remaining time and status, which may appear on the lock screen. They contain no wellbeing notes or exercise names.

Retention, deletion and export

Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app and its local data; existing backups and cloud copies are separate. Manage entries in their respective views. “Delete all training data” removes training content but does not completely erase all settings, cloud data or backups. With sync enabled, deletions may reach other devices at the next successful sync. Also manage cloud data in iCloud settings. Secure and delete exported files separately. JSON import creates a new set and does not restore all data.

Website and voluntary support

The website uses no advertising or analytics trackers of its own and reads no app training data. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. External links open services with their own privacy information. Feedback opens an editable email draft with app version, build, iOS version and device type; training or Health data is not automatically attached. Support receives your message and sender address only when you send it yourself. Please do not send health data. Support messages are retained as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary documentation or comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and browser storage

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. If you allow it, this tab remembers your language and learning checklist ticks until you close it. These settings are not sent to us.

Providers, international processing and security

Apple provides iCloud, Health and the device platform; GitHub hosts the website. Apple and GitHub describe international processing, including in the United States, and safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or applicable privacy frameworks in their privacy policies. Timer does not offer a choice or guarantee of storage exclusively in Switzerland or the EU. Local data relies on device security; the app adds no end-to-end encryption of its own. Protect your devices, Apple Account and exported files.

Your rights and optional permissions

Depending on applicable law, rights include access, rectification, deletion, data portability and, where applicable, restriction, objection and complaints to a competent data protection authority. Contact the developer. The developer has no central copy of content held exclusively on your device or in your private Apple Account for handling your request. Disable optional features in the app or system settings; this does not automatically undo previous processing or remove existing copies. Where the GDPR applies, necessary service provision relies on contract performance, optional processing requiring consent relies on consent, and necessary support or security relies on legitimate interests or legal obligations, as applicable.

First launch and changes

On first launch, the app displays usage notes and a link to this statement. You accept the usage notes and acknowledge the privacy information. The app stores the acknowledgement and its time locally and does not include them in its iCloud sync. This does not enable optional features. The information remains available in Help & Information. Material changes are published here with a new date.

Annual Premium subscription through Apple

The optional Premium annual subscription is handled through Apple StoreKit. It renews automatically each year unless cancelled with Apple at least 24 hours before expiry. Apple displays the current annual price before checkout and handles payment and the App Store account. Timer checks verified subscription entitlements and honours existing verified lifetime purchases. Manage or cancel under Apple Settings → Subscriptions. Timer has no payment server and does not collect card details. Subscription status is not part of training backups or private CloudKit synchronization. Apple terms and privacy notices also apply.

Local acknowledgements

Under Help → Privacy & acknowledgements, the app stores accepted usage notices and acknowledgement of privacy information locally, including text copies, checksums, language, app version, build, device time and time zone. Older acknowledgements retain only information actually available. The log contains no training or wellbeing data, enables no optional permissions, and is not transferred through app iCloud sync or training backups. It is excluded from automatic device backups. You can export or delete it separately; exported copies remain where saved. App deletion or device loss can remove it. Device time and checksums are not independent identity or time evidence.

Privacy & acknowledgements

Sync mode and device activity

In Always mode, training data syncs when the app launches, when you finish a workout and during use once at least an hour has passed since the last sync. In Manual mode, training data syncs on request. The icon to the left of the eye in the training overview and “Sync now” in “Reminders & iCloud” start a sync in either mode. In both modes, startup warnings automatically process a random local installation identifier, general device kind (iPhone/iPad), foreground status and CloudKit modification times in your private iCloud database. Activity reports contain no personal device name or training content. The identifier is not transferred in training backups. The status record holds the last status, not an activity history; older cloud status records may remain until cloud data is deleted. An active report expires after 150 seconds without a heartbeat. Backgrounding attempts to mark inactive; disabling sync attempts to remove this device’s cloud activity report. Warnings require connectivity and support on both devices, do not lock devices and are not independent evidence of use. Running timers and the local acknowledgement log remain outside sync.

The optional sync history contains only timestamps, statuses and the type of sync. It stays on this device for the selected 1 to 10 days and is excluded from Timer iCloud sync and exported training backups.

Synchronisation and device activity

Local checkpoint and display options

To resume training, Timer stores a local checkpoint containing exercise text, sequence, position, captured time segments, session identifier and control state. The file uses iOS file protection until first unlock, is excluded from device backups and is neither synced via iCloud nor included in training or set exports. Finishing without a pending choice, Discard, Reset and deleting all local training data remove this checkpoint; Discard preserves completed history entries. Duration, music on/off and hiding personal information are saved as local preferences. Hiding changes only the display and widget cache, not stored data or explicitly requested exports. Undo information stays in memory only while editing. These features introduce no new data transfer.
