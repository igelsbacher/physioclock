iCloud: what is synced?

Optional iCloud sync is available in both versions.

This overview describes PhysioClock sync in version 3.32.2, Build 83. Sync is optional and uses the private iCloud database of the same Apple Account. Enable it in the app on both devices. A running timer is not transferred to the other device.

When and how does sync run?

In Always mode, training data syncs when the app launches, when you finish a workout and during use once at least an hour has passed since the last sync.

In Manual mode, training data syncs on request. The icon to the left of the eye in the training overview and “Sync now” in “Reminders & iCloud” start a sync in either mode.

Training data sync waits until you finish the workout and resolve any extra steps or recovery prompts. In Manual mode, you can then start a sync yourself.

Concurrent changes and deletions

Sets, history entries, custom catalogue entries and wellbeing merge by identifier. For the same entry, the newer saved change time wins; source identifiers break ties. The current exercise list and other individual settings are handled as whole values. Fields within a set, goal, weekly plan or reminder list are not merged individually. Different device clocks can affect ordering.

Deletions and technical change data also transfer: entry identifiers, change times, random source identifiers and deletion markers. Deleted entries can therefore disappear on the other device at its next sync. Switching sync off does not delete existing training data in iCloud. Sync does not replace a separate backup file.

Separate device-activity check

With sync enabled, including Manual mode, the information notice is followed by automatic processing of a random installation identifier, iPhone/iPad type, foreground status and CloudKit timestamps in the private iCloud database. A registry contains installation identifiers. Activity messages contain no personal device name, exercise or wellbeing note. Status refreshes about every 45 seconds and expires after 150 seconds without a heartbeat. A warning is not a device lock or independent usage proof. Internet and support on both devices are required.

Moving to the background attempts to publish inactivity. Switching sync off attempts to remove this device’s activity record. Network errors can prevent this; active status still expires. Removing this status is separate from retaining training data.

Example: iPhone and iPad

A set saved on iPhone appears on iPad after successful sync. Its language, font and speech switch stay unchanged. A timer with 03:20 remaining and a local recovery checkpoint do not transfer. A wellbeing entry with its note does transfer. Changed A/B/C sliders stay local; an exercise list loaded from them does not.

This list covers PhysioClock sync only. Apple Health sync, device backups, iCloud Drive and purchase management are separate Apple functions. “Not transferred by this sync” does not mean a file or setting can never reach another device through an Apple feature you enabled.

Information applies to this version. Use the same current app version on both devices for comparable behaviour. Some displays and permissions depend on the device and iOS.

Sync log

Under “Help & Information → Settings & iCloud → Settings → Sync log”, choose whether to show the log, whether to save history and how long to keep it. Find the log and saved history in “Reminders & iCloud”.

The log shows the current or most recent sync of this app session. If you save history, sync times and statuses stay on this device for the selected 1 to 10 days. Turning off history storage deletes the saved history.

Your connection, Apple Account, data volume or concurrent edits can delay sync. “Completed” refers to this device. The other device needs its own sync. If needed, sync the device with the change first.
