iCloud and synchronisation

iCloud sync is optional. Choose Off, Manual or Always under Reminders & iCloud. Always syncs on launch, after completion and during use no sooner than after one hour.

Manual sync starts using Sync now. A running or paused timer blocks sync; active timers are not transferred between devices.

Neutral and movement data are stored in separate records in the same private iCloud database. The mode switch and individual name are local.

The optional sync log contains timestamps, status and sync type and remains local for 1 to 10 days. Activity warnings are not a lock. Sync may take time.
