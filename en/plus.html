Free base version & PhysioClock Premium

iCloud sync is available with PhysioClock Premium.

Both display modes are free: Simple and Regular. Premium adds features through a one-time in-app purchase.

Premium is a one-time unlock. The price shown in the app for your App Store before purchase applies. No subscription or advertising.

Premium is unlocked in the app through Apple. This website does not sell an unlock.

The free base version lets you save one set and keep editing it. Additional new sets require Premium. Existing extra sets are not deleted or locked when you update.

One-time purchase & restoration

In the app: Help → Settings & iCloud → PhysioClock Premium. Find the current price and Restore purchases there. Your App Store account is used to verify the unlock.

Existing sets, exercises and history stay accessible and usable. Without Premium, you can create one saved set; existing extra sets are not deleted. Previously enabled speech, displays and reminders from older versions are kept on this device. Purchase status is not included in training backups or app iCloud sync.

Backup, full restoration and deletion stay free. Importing an additional set requires a free set slot or Premium.

Enjoy your training! PhysioClock Premium removes this notice and enables iCloud sync.
