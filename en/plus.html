PhysioClock Premium

Free

The free version includes 30 training sessions per installation. Each new session counts once when you start it. Pausing, resuming and cooling down belong to the same session. The walkthrough is free.

At launch, the app shows how many of your 30 free sessions are available. Workouts from before this update are excluded.

Premium

A one-time PhysioClock Premium purchase unlocks unlimited sessions. Both versions include every feature, including iCloud, sets, templates and reminders.

Your saved workouts, sets and goals remain accessible. Deleting training history or importing a backup leaves the local session count in place.

One-time purchase in the app

The displayed price for your App Store applies. Apple processes the purchase.
