Timer Premium

The first five started sessions are free. Further starts require an active annual subscription; pausing and resuming the same session does not consume another start.

Apple displays the purchase price. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before expiry. Manage it in Apple settings.

Saved programmes and history are not automatically removed when a subscription ends. Use restore purchases in the app if needed.
