Help resources

Help and replay

Open Getting started & controls → Quick tips & guide to find the PDF guide and replay this introduction. You can open and share the PDF guide in the app.

Walkthrough and icons

Try the timer controls in the walkthrough. Open the icon guide to learn what the symbols mean.

Back up and restore

Find backups under Help & Information → Privacy & backups. Export a backup or restore your training data from an existing backup.

App configuration

Display mode

Choose Simple for larger controls or Regular for the full view. You can switch modes later. Change the display mode under Settings.

Startup photo

The woman at the window appears each time the app launches, in portrait and landscape. The default is 5 seconds. Choose 0 to 9 seconds. Select “Off” (0 seconds) to skip the photo. This setting is available in Simple and Full mode.

Help & Information → Settings & iCloud → Settings → Display → Startup photo

App language

Open Help & Information → Settings & iCloud → Language. Choose German, English, French or Italian. Your own exercise names keep their original spelling.

Edit exercises

In Regular mode, tap the sliders icon to open the editor. Adjust the names, durations and order, then save your changes.

Exercise catalogue

In the training editor, tap Choose exercises from catalogue. Browse exercises by body area or add your own.

Training sets

The stack icon opens your saved sets. Save the current exercise sequence as a set and load it for your next workout.

Templates A, B and C

Adjust a template and apply it as the programme for your next workout. You’ll find the templates in the set picker.

Sound and vibration

In Regular mode, open Signals in the training editor to find sound and vibration. Choose your exercise-change cues and adjust their volume.

Rounds and rests

Set both values in the training editor’s sequence options. Set how often the sequence repeats and how long the rests last.

Reminders

Open Reminders & iCloud and save the times you chose. Choose times and weekdays for up to three reminders.

iCloud sync

Turn on iCloud in Reminders & iCloud. Choose automatic or manual mode. Use the same Apple Account on both devices to sync.

Widget and Lock Screen

In Regular mode, enable the widget or the Live Activity for the Lock Screen.. Find both switches under Settings → Widget and Lock Screen.

Training

Start training

Open the training view and tap Start. The triangle is the Start button. The countdown shows the time left in the current exercise.

Pause and resume

Tap Pause to stop the timer temporarily. Resume continues the same session where you left off.

Move between exercises

Go back to the previous exercise or tap Next to skip the remaining time. The arrows with a vertical bar sit alongside the timer controls.

Halfway cue

Open Settings → Training cues → Halfway cue to make your choice. Choose a halfway announcement, a short beep or silence.

Optional exercises

After the required exercises, decide whether to add the optional exercises. Mark individual exercises as optional in the editor.

Cool-down

After training, choose a cool-down phase and set its duration. Tap Yes to start the selected duration. The timer shows Cool-down.

Meditative cool-down

Choose a quiet cool-down and turn on music if you wish. Use the Music switch to turn the soundtrack on or off.

Summary

Review your session summary. Tap Next to return to the training overview. The summary shows your recorded active time.

Overviews

Training overview

Continue to training opens the timer. Tap Start to begin.

Personal time goal

Tap the goal card in the training overview. Set a daily or weekly goal in minutes and track your progress.

Monthly overview / Goals

The calendar icon opens the monthly overview. Review your training minutes in the calendar and use the colours to see your progress.

Training diary

The calendar-and-clock icon opens the training diary. Review saved sessions or enable duration corrections in Settings.

Wellbeing

Record how you feel and review your entries over time. Choose one of the five levels and save your entry.

Personal details

Use the eye button to hide or show personal details in the overview. A crossed-out eye means personal details are hidden.

Weekly plan

Open a day and tap Add. Schedule one or more saved sets for each day of the week.

Sync manually

Tap the sync icon to the left of the eye in the training overview. Find the optional log in Reminders & iCloud. The sync button appears when iCloud sync is on.

Free sessions

The free version includes 30 training starts per installation. The overview shows how many are left. Premium removes the limit on new training starts.
