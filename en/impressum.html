Developer and legal information

PhysioClock is developed by Heinz Josef Igelsbacher. It helps organise self-selected movement exercises and does not replace medical diagnosis, professional advice or individually prescribed physiotherapy. Follow advice appropriate to your health situation. Stop exercising and seek professional assistance if you experience pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms. Liability is limited only insofar as permitted by applicable law.
